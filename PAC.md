For High School PAC's (Parent Advisary Committee's) to help bring the meta-course Maker100 Leaders Robotics (https://github.com/hpssjellis/maker100-leaders-robotics/tree/main) to their High School by making it an advanced technology course inside any computer lab course, 
for either an existing robotics or electronics course to for any computer lab course that wants 7 driven math/physics capable students to attempt before running a full course.

For example 7 driven students in a webpage design course could start the course with the PAC raising $2000 USD. 
(2026 price list [here]([/price-list-2026.html](https://hpssjellis.github.io/maker100-leaders-robotics/price-list-2026.html) Note: Change the number of students to 7.)
This course is a meta-course since it allows hands on using of concepts from Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Communication Skills and Peer teaching.
It uses, no specialized educator since the students do all the problem solving. The base hardware changes rapidly meaning the course will always have new concepts to solve, The educator must be aware of safety, and suggestions.  the analog firewall of code handwritten summaries and drawn circuit diagrams and to insure learning, and 
Teach Three to pass on discoveries. LLM's like chatGPT or private local versions are used to help problem solve just like in real life, 
but the ~50 assignments still neede to physically work, before final projects are began.


The curriculum is at  https://github.com/hpssjellis/maker100-curriculum and may evolve but the basic concepts wont change even as cheaper lower power, faster better core hardware is available
Most of the supporting hardware sense and actuators do not dramatically change. Interrelated sets are discouraged because they often remove the connections which are the education.
